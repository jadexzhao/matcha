# Marketing skeleton · Private Car Sale

## Channel mix

- Facebook / Marketplace listing (primary)
- Craigslist or local classifieds (same copy block)
- Optional one-page HTML listing (`web/index.html`) for a clean share link
- Short SMS / email reply template for serious enquiries

## Listing caption skeleton

Hook (one line): Clean private sale ... [year] [make] [model], [mileage] miles.

What it is (two to four lines): Condition summary, recent service if known, must-know caveats, title status in plain language.

How to act: Message or email with a viewing window that works for you. Serious enquiries only.

Sign-off: Jade🪴 supports listing clarity and ops ... not dealer advertising.

## Photo shot list (ops)

1. Front three-quarter
2. Rear three-quarter
3. Driver side / passenger side
4. Interior front seats
5. Dash + odometer (readable)
6. Tyres / wheels
7. Known scratch or dent (honest)
8. Under-bonnet (optional)

## Reply template (serious enquiry)

Thanks for writing. Happy to arrange a viewing. Please share a day/time window and whether you need to see service records on site. Cheers, [seller initial only]
